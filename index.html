Health and Retirement StudyIn progress

Does income change depression risk in older adults?

A lagged causal forest on NIH-funded HRS data (n=5,106, Wave 14 to 15) testing whether household income causally affects depression risk.

No significant average effect, but significant heterogeneity by education and functional status, confirmed with a best linear projection.

R · grf · causal forest · Shannon, Choi & Hites

30 Years of Rural Medical Education

Since 1996, the Rural Medical Scholars Program has trained future physicians with one goal: more doctors practicing in rural Alabama. Thirty years of outcomes lived in a hand-kept spreadsheet, with the same status, specialty, or school written a dozen different ways.

Dr. Shabnam Naher and I built a reproducible R Markdown report that cleans those records, places every scholar on the path from entry to practice, and flags any value it doesn't recognize. When new outcomes come in, the whole report updates itself.

87 of the program's 295 scholars now practice in rural Alabama, and most of them are family doctors. But newer graduates are less likely to end up in rural towns than earlier ones. For every 10 years later a scholar started, the odds of practicing in a rural town dropped by about 43% (logistic regression, p = .03). That gives the program an early warning it can act on.

R · R Markdown · dplyr · ggplot2 · plotly · Cochran-Armitage · logistic regression

25 Years of MOORES Scholars

MOORES (Medical Opportunities and Outreach, Rural Education Scholars) supports students from rural Alabama, most from the Black Belt. Dr. Shabnam Naher and I recoded 25 years of hand-entered records into one row per student. We traced all 256 students through the Office's other pipeline programs and built a Shiny app so staff can make their own crosstabs.

33%

Dissertation · HSLS:09Complete

Which parental involvement moves STEM grades?

Causal mediation (RMPW) on 23,000+ students followed from 9th to 12th grade, testing six parent behaviors with STEM self-efficacy as the mediator.

Parent educational aspirations had the largest direct effect (d = 0.57). None of the effects ran through self-efficacy.

R · RMPW · sensitivity analysis

Does the questionnaire mean the same thing to everyone?

Rasch modeling and measurement invariance testing on a federal parental involvement survey of 25,000+ families.

Flagged specific items that functioned differently across racial and socioeconomic groups.

Paint on water

The work that doesn't need a p-value. In this style of painting, pigment is floated on a thickened water bath, raked and combed into patterns, then lifted onto paper. What I love is that each pattern exists once, since lifting the sheet takes the paint with it. As Bob Ross put it, there are no mistakes, just happy little accidents.
